## Active scene, version 2

The site now uses `cave-grounded-v2.webp` (408 KB) and `cave-interior-v2.webp` (416 KB). Both were created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-26, using the original cave plate and the original `kultegin.jpg` and `bilge-khagan.jpg` as references. Stones, lighting, gravel occlusion and contact shadows are integrated into each image. The entrance zooms toward its passage and blends into the interior chamber, which continues moving inward. The previous independently animated stone cutouts are no longer rendered.

These are artistic scenes, including their inscriptions; they must not be treated as exact epigraphic transcriptions or documentation of stones found in Teshik-Tash. Original museum photographs remain available in the reduced-motion reading layout and their existing credits. The generated originals remain in the image-generation library; the project consumes compressed WebP copies.

Realistic human scale, no huge sculptures, no repetitions, no floating, no rectangular cutout backgrounds. Keep the center path and center-right dark passage empty and inviting so a camera can go INTO it. The dark passage entrance is large enough to walk into, centered at around x=65%,y=52%. Keep lower-left 35% dark and uncluttered for website typography. Wide 16:9 photographic plate, no people, no torches, no labels, no modern objects. Do not add decorative fake glyphs; retain the dense worn inscription character of the actual stone references. Goal: utterly coherent documentary-style lighting and photographic material detail, not a 3D render.

Absolutely no people, no trees, no buildings, no lamps, no inscriptions, no standing steles, no text. The lower-left 35% is dark unobtrusive rock for overlaid site typography, the right side has beautifully legible rock structure. Deep photographic perspective, no mirrored or repeated walls. Deliver a wide landscape image.

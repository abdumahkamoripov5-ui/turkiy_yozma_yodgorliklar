# Original stone photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-26. All three photographs are released into the public domain by the authors on their file pages. Original files retained; the interface crops and transforms their display with CSS. These are photographs, not reconstructed 3D scans.

- `kultegin.jpg`: Betta27, 4 August 2010. [Original and public-domain declaration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kultegin_monument_Mongolia.JPG).
- `bilge-khagan.jpg`: Betta27, 4 August 2010. [Original and public-domain declaration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bilge_Khagan_monument_Mongolia.JPG).
- `kultegin-detail.jpg`: Betta27, derivative crop by Hunnjazal. [Original, crop details and public-domain declaration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kultegin_monument_Mongolia_section.jpg).

In-page credits link to the same sources. The preview does not label decorative or unverified runic strings as scholarly transcriptions.

`kultegin-sculpture.webp` and `bilge-sculpture.webp` are transparent, prelit artistic renderings made from these same public-domain photographs and a procedural stone silhouette. Lighting and photographic projection were computed offline, then exported from the local WebGL scene. The preview animates these assets with CSS transforms so no WebGL rendering or shader compilation is required while scrolling. They are not archaeological scans.
